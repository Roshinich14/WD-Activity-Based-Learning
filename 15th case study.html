Essential Yoga Poses

Build strength, flexibility, and inner harmony with our foundational posture guide.

Beginner

Tree Pose (Vrikshasana)

Improves balance, concentration, and stability while strengthening thighs, calves, ankles, and spine.

Intermediate

Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II)

Builds stamina, opens the hips and chest, and enhances focus and coordination across the entire body.

All Levels

Lotus Pose (Padmasana)

A classic meditative posture that calms the brain, stretches knees and ankles, and promotes upright posture.

Meditation Techniques

Cultivate calm, reduce stress, and quiet mental chatter with proven mindfulness practices.

Mindfulness (Vipassana)

Focusing on the natural breath to anchor your awareness in the present moment without judgment or distraction.

Loving-Kindness (Metta)

Directing unconditional goodwill and compassion toward yourself, loved ones, and eventually all living beings.

Pranayama (Breathwork)

Controlled breathing exercises designed to purify subtle energy channels and harmonize the nervous system.
